# High-resolution / fine-grained-detail VQA datasets with public labels (for fine-tuning Qwen2.5-VL 3B/7B toward VisualOverload)

Scope: datasets whose answers depend on small details in large or dense images, with published labels. Checked as of 2026-10-15. Licenses, split sizes and fields were read directly from the Hugging Face Hub API, the datasets-server `/info` endpoint and the dataset cards, unless a finding says otherwise. "Viewer rows" means the row count that HF datasets-server reports, which can differ from the paper's count.

Reference target: VisualOverload has 2,720 QA pairs in a single `test` split. Its ground truth is private and scored through an evaluation server. Most images match the pixel count of 4K (3840x2160). `question_type` is one of choice (A–D or A–B), counting (freeform) or ocr (freeform). The categories are activity, attributes, counting, ocr, reasoning and scene, and each question has an easy/medium/hard difficulty. The dataset is CC BY-SA 4.0 and the images are public-domain (CC0) artwork — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/paulgavrikov/visualoverload). The 150 paintings come from museum collections on Google Arts & Culture — [arXiv 2509.25339](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.25339). The images themselves are downloadable in the test parquet (only the labels are private), so dataset-level deduplication against them is possible — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/paulgavrikov/visualoverload).

## Q1. What datasets exist? (name, venue/year, links, size, resolution)

### Takeaway
About 35 relevant labelled datasets exist. They fall into five groups: (a) natural-image "visual search / zoom" training sets from 2024–2026 (SEAL/V*-train, DeepEyes-47k, TreeVGR-RL-37K, VisualProbe, ZwZ-RL-VQA, FineRS-4k, Visual-CoT, CoF, PixelReasoner, Thyme); (b) high-resolution benchmarks with public labels that are only safe as dev sets (V*, HR-Bench 4K/8K, MME-RealWorld/-Lite/-CN, TreeBench, ZoomBench, HRScene val/testmini); (c) remote-sensing ultra-high-resolution sets, including real training data (GeoLLaVA-8K SuperRS/HighRS-VQA, UHR-BAT-SFT-10K, FIT-RS, GeoChat-Instruct, VRSBench, HRVQA) and benchmarks (XLRS-Bench, LRS-VQA, RSHR-Bench, GEOBench-VLM); (d) general perception benchmarks (MMStar, BLINK, CV-Bench, HallusionBench, SEED-Bench-2-Plus, MMVP), which are mostly low resolution; (e) gigapixel material, which exists only as benchmark slices (HRScene-PANDA, UR-Bench). I found no labelled training VQA set built on PANDA.

### Cited Findings

#### (a) Natural-image fine-grained / visual-search TRAINING data

- **SEAL VQA instruction data (V\* training data)**, CVPR 2024 (V\*). HF: [craigwu/seal_vqa_data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/craigwu/seal_vqa_data). Files: GQA_data, llava_focus_data, llava_instruct_data, negative_data, spatial_relation_data, vaw_attribute_data (from the HF file listing). The paper gives 387k samples in total: 100k negative target-object reasoning samples (COCO2017, questions generated by GPT-3.5 about absent or very small objects), 70k GQA, 51k object attributes from VAW, 46k spatial relations from COCO2017, and 120k LLaVA (LLaVA-80K plus 40k COCO-matched) — [arXiv 2312.14135v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14135v2). The images come from COCO-2014/2017 and GQA, downloaded separately — [V\* GitHub README](https://github.com/penghao-wu/vstar). The repo is MIT-licensed — [V\* GitHub](https://github.com/penghao-wu/vstar).
- **DeepEyes-Datasets-47k** (DeepEyes, arXiv 2505.14362, 2025). HF: [ChenShawn/DeepEyes-Datasets-47k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ChenShawn/DeepEyes-Datasets-47k), Apache-2.0 (HF tag). It contains "Visual Search (47%, 22k samples)", which is the V\* training set derived from COCO2017; ArxivQA (30%, 14k); and ThinkLite-VL (23%, 11k). Filtering removes samples that Qwen2.5-VL-7B solves at 0% or 100% accuracy, converts every question to open-ended form, and keeps only fine-grained samples that are solvable with the ground-truth region — [arXiv 2505.14362](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14362).
- **TreeVGR-RL-37K / TreeVGR-SFT-35K** (TreeVGR/TreeBench, arXiv 2507.07999, 2025). HF: [HaochenWang/TreeVGR-RL-37K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeVGR-RL-37K) has 36,752 viewer rows with fields `images, problem, answer, target_instances`. Its data file is named `vstar30k_visdrone6k_x1y1x2y2.parquet`, i.e. roughly 30k V\* training items plus 6k VisDrone items with boxes. Apache-2.0 (HF tag/API). [HaochenWang/TreeVGR-SFT-35K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeVGR-SFT-35K) is a single JSON file under Apache-2.0. The TreeVGR model, initialized from Qwen2.5-VL-7B, gains +16.8 on V\*, +12.6 on MME-RealWorld and +13.4 on TreeBench — [arXiv 2507.07999 via HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeBench).
- **VisualProbe** (Mini-o3, arXiv 2509.07969, 2025). The paper says it has "4,000 visual question–answer pairs for training and 500 pairs for testing", at easy/medium/hard levels, built around small targets, many distractor objects and high-resolution images. Answers need semantic matching, so the reward comes from an LLM judge — [arXiv 2509.07969](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07969). A 2026 paper describes it as "4K-resolution images with small targets, dense distractors" — [arXiv 2607.00816](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00816). HF: [Mini-o3/VisualProbe_train](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mini-o3/VisualProbe_train) (2,900 viewer rows, image-folder layout, no license tag). [Mini-o3/VisualProbe_Hard](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Mini-o3/VisualProbe_Hard) has 106 rows with fields `images, doc_id, problem, solution`, and there are Easy and Medium splits too. **Conflict:** the paper says 4,000 train items, the HF viewer shows 2,900 rows, and a third-party repo is called `dddraxxx/visualprobe-train-5.7k`, so the train count is unreconciled.
- **ZwZ-RL-VQA** ("Zooming without Zooming" / Region-to-Image Distillation, ICML 2026, arXiv 2602.11858). HF: [inclusionAI/ZwZ-RL-VQA](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZwZ-RL-VQA), Apache-2.0. Source images come from SA-1B, LAION, MetaCLIP, Visual Genome, CC12M and STPLS3D and are "mostly > 1000×1000". Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B wrote the questions on micro-crops and Qwen3-VL-235B plus GLM-4.5V answered them. Items were kept only with >75% teacher agreement and only if Qwen3-VL-8B gets them right less than 50% of the time. Bounding boxes are overlaid on the full image. Fields: `images, original_images, bbox, problem, answer, ability, data_source` — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZwZ-RL-VQA); [GitHub](https://github.com/inclusionAI/Zooming-without-Zooming). **Conflict:** the card says "37k" samples but the viewer shows 110,988 train rows.
- **FineRS-4k** (FineRS, NeurIPS 2025, arXiv 2510.21311). It uses 4K drone video frames ("3840 × 2060") from YouTube and the authors' own UAV footage, split 8,956 train / 749 val / 2,427 test. Tasks are instruction-guided segmentation, multiple-choice VQA and open-ended VQA. Objects are tiny: S >0.055% of image area, XS 0.017–0.055%, XXS <0.017% — [arXiv 2510.21311](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21311v1); [NeurIPS poster](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2025/poster/115465). HF: [Jiazuo98/Finers-4k-benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Jiazuo98/Finers-4k-benchmark) ships `all_annotations_final_{train,val,test}_v5.json` and `all_images.zip`. The license tag is only "cc", with no variant given.
- **Visual-CoT / VisCoT** (NeurIPS 2024 Datasets & Benchmarks, Spotlight, arXiv 2403.16999). 438k QA pairs, each with a bounding box for the key region, and about 98k with reasoning steps — [NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/0ff38d72a2e0aa6dbe42de83a17b2223-Abstract.html); [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.16999v3). HF: [deepcs233/Visual-CoT](https://huggingface.co/datasets/deepcs233/Visual-CoT), Apache-2.0. It provides `viscot_363k.json` (VisCoT-only data) and `viscot_mixed_2m.json`, covering text/doc, fine-grained (e.g. CUB), charts, general VQA and relation reasoning — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/deepcs233/Visual-CoT). **Conflict:** the paper says 438k but the released list is 363k. One possible explanation is that the remainder are benchmark items.
- **Chain-of-Focus (CoF)** (arXiv 2505.15436, 2025). [xintongzhang/CoF-SFT-Data-5.4k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xintongzhang/CoF-SFT-Data-5.4k) has 5.4k samples (2.4k with zoom-in, 3k text-only) and is Apache-2.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xintongzhang/CoF-SFT-Data-5.4k). [xintongzhang/CoF-RL-Data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xintongzhang/CoF-RL-Data) has 8,853 train and 9 test rows, includes a `reward_model` / `groundtruth_complete` field, and has no license tag.
- **Pixel Reasoner data** (arXiv 2505.15966). [TIGER-Lab/PixelReasoner-RL-Data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/PixelReasoner-RL-Data) has 15,402 queries with verifiable answers covering infographics, visually rich scenes and video. [PixelReasoner-SFT-Data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/PixelReasoner-SFT-Data) has 8,000+ trajectories. Both are Apache-2.0 — [HF cards](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/PixelReasoner-RL-Data).
- **Thyme** (arXiv 2508.11630, Kwai, 2025). [Kwai-Keye/Thyme-RL](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Kwai-Keye/Thyme-RL) has 55,173 rows with fields `question, solution, images`. [Kwai-Keye/Thyme-SFT](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Kwai-Keye/Thyme-SFT) has splits 2round 152,354, computation 12,342 and wo_thinking_single_round 181,277. Both are MIT (HF tag/viewer). The card says Thyme targets "high-resolution perception and complex reasoning" through code execution — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Kwai-Keye/Thyme-RL).
- **MGPO training data** (arXiv 2507.05920, Findings of ACL 2026). Qwen2.5-VL-7B was post-trained on 21K samples taken from MME-RealWorld (Apache-2.0), with no grounding annotations. V\* was used out of distribution — [arXiv 2507.05920](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.05920) (via search summary). I found no confirmation that the processed 21K split itself was released. A third-party HF repo [xinyu1205/mme_realworld_train_21690](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xinyu1205/mme_realworld_train_21690) exists (9,720 viewer rows; fields `question, multi-choice options, answer, category, image`) and is likely related (unverified).
- **Q-Zoom-Training** (arXiv 2604.06912, 2026). [YuhengSSS/Q-Zoom-Training](https://huggingface.co/datasets/YuhengSSS/Q-Zoom-Training), Apache-2.0. It mixes GQA 72K, OCR-VQA 80K, VCoT-DocVQA 33K, TextVQA 34K, ChartQA 28K, VCoT-InfoVQA 15K, V\*-COCO 44K and VCoT-TextVQA+GQA 68K, with separate files for Qwen2.5-VL 3B and 7B — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/YuhengSSS/Q-Zoom-Training).
- **DeepEyesV2 data**: [honglyhly/DeepEyesV2_SFT](https://huggingface.co/datasets/honglyhly/DeepEyesV2_SFT) and [DeepEyesV2_RL](https://huggingface.co/datasets/honglyhly/DeepEyesV2_RL). The RL files are `perception_all_1..5.parquet`, `reason.parquet`, `search.parquet` and `vstar_test.parquet`. No license tag and no card (HF API).

#### (b) High-resolution benchmarks with public labels (dev/eval only)

- **V\* Bench** (CVPR 2024). 191 SA-1B images at an average of 2246×1582, with 115 attribute-recognition and 76 spatial-relationship questions. Multiple choice with 4 options (attribute) or 2 options (spatial) — [arXiv 2312.14135v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14135v2). HF: [craigwu/vstar_bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/craigwu/vstar_bench) (no license tag; the repo is MIT).
- **HR-Bench 4K / 8K** (from the DC² paper, arXiv 2408.15556; AAAI 2025 per common citation, unverified). Two task families: FSP (attribute recognition, OCR, visual prompting) and FCP (maps, charts, spatial relations). The 8K version averages 8K resolution; the 4K version is cropped from it around annotated object coordinates — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DreamMr/HR-Bench). The HF license tag is "other" and the LICENSE file is 0 bytes — [HF LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DreamMr/HR-Bench/blob/main/LICENSE).
- **MME-RealWorld** (ICLR 2025, arXiv 2408.13257). 13,366 images averaging 2,000×1,500 and 29,429 human-annotated QA across 43 tasks in 5 scenarios (OCR in the wild, remote sensing, diagram/table, monitoring, autonomous driving). Remote-sensing images average more than 5000×5000, and one monitoring item asks for a count of 133 vehicles. MME-RealWorld-CN adds 5,917 QA — [GitHub README](https://github.com/yfzhang114/MME-RealWorld). HF: [yifanzhang114/MME-RealWorld](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yifanzhang114/MME-RealWorld) (Apache-2.0 tag) and [MME-RealWorld-Lite](https://huggingface.co/datasets/yifanzhang114/MME-RealWorld-Lite) (50 samples per task, no license tag).
- **TreeBench** (arXiv 2507.07999). 405 MC questions on dense SA-1B images, written by 8 experts and each paired with a bounding box. Subcategories: Attributes, Material, Physical State, Object Retrieval, OCR, Perspective Transform, Ordering, Contact/Occlusion, Spatial Containment, Comparison. o3 scores 54.87 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeBench). Apache-2.0.
- **ZoomBench** (ICML 2026). 845 test items in MCQ and open-question format across 6 perceptual dimensions. Generated by Gemini-2.5-Pro and verified by humans, with boxes and crop views. Qwen2.5-VL-7B gets 57.5% wrong — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZoomBench). Apache-2.0.
- **HRScene** (Penn State NLP; venue unverified). 7,073 samples from 25 real-world plus 2 synthetic datasets, with resolutions from 1,024×1,024 to 35,503×26,627. Splits: val 750, testmini 1,000 and test 5,323. Test labels are not released and go through an online evaluator — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wenliang04/HRScene). License is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 — [HF LICENSE](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wenliang04/HRScene). Real-world subsets include `realworld_PANDA`, `realworld_ArtBench`, `realworld_VStar_Bench`, `realworld_HR-Bench`, `realworld_HRVQA`, `realworld_Remote_Sensing`, `realworld_Video_Monitoring` and others, each with about 30 validation items (datasets-server `/info`).
- **UR-Bench** (arXiv 2601.08748, 2026). Ultra-high-resolution images: Chinese scroll paintings from Wikipedia averaging 48,685×2,821 (up to 718 MP), plus SUNet satellite and Google Street View panoramas. Questions are 4-option single choice at Micro, Regional and Global levels. Release, labels and license are not stated in the HTML — [arXiv 2601.08748v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08748v1).

#### (c) Remote-sensing / aerial ultra-high-resolution sets

- **GeoLLaVA-8K: SuperRS-VQA + HighRS-VQA** (NeurIPS 2025, arXiv 2505.21375). SuperRS-VQA averages 8,376×8,378 and HighRS-VQA averages 2,000×1,912. Together they hold **81,367 UHR image–text pairs used for SFT**, over 22 dialogue tasks, deduplicated against existing RS datasets — [GitHub README](https://github.com/MiliLab/GeoLLaVA-8K); [NeurIPS 2025](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/e95ef5c6ed4096c609e0b8b47ffaeb9b-Abstract-Conference.html). HF: [initiacms/GeoLLaVA-Data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/initiacms/GeoLLaVA-Data), tagged VQA/QA/multiple-choice with no license tag. A third-party repo, [Anonymous-BdjkruUUIg/UHR-CoZ](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Anonymous-BdjkruUUIg/UHR-CoZ), repackages HighRS with chain-of-zoom JSON (no card).
- **XLRS-Bench** (CVPR 2025 Highlight). Average size 8,500×8,500, with 840 images at 10,000×10,000. 45,942 annotations over 16 tasks (10 perception, 6 reasoning). VQA is MC (A–D), and land-use classification is multi-answer — [GitHub README](https://github.com/AI9Stars/XLRS-Bench). HF: [initiacms/XLRS-Bench-lite](https://huggingface.co/datasets/initiacms/XLRS-Bench-lite) has 420 VQA rows in a split named "train", even though it is a benchmark. Captioning and grounding are released in full. Annotations are CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and the images inherit DOTA/Google Earth academic-only terms and ISPRS Toronto no-redistribution terms — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/initiacms/XLRS-Bench-lite).
- **UHR-BAT-SFT-10K** (RL-MIND, 2026). 10,000 SFT examples, 2,422 unique images and 17.17 GiB. Images include DOTA-2 tiles. Fields: `question, answer, first_image_width/height, source_dataset`. The license is declared "other" with no name given — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/RL-MIND/UHR-BAT-SFT-10K).
- **RSHR-Bench** (arXiv 2512.17319). 1,932 test examples (210 GiB of parquet). Covers MC and open VQA, grounding, counting, color, shape, and multi-image/multi-turn temporal and anomaly tasks. Apache-2.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/RL-MIND/RSHR-Bench). XHRBench is a sibling package.
- **LRS-VQA** (arXiv 2503.07588). 1,657 images with side lengths from 1,024 to 27,328 px and 7,333 QA in 8 categories. Images come from FAIR1M train, GLH-Bridge test and STAR test. CC BY-NC 4.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ll-13/LRS-VQA).
- **FIT-RS** (SkySenseGPT). 1,800,851 fine-grained RS instruction samples covering relationships among objects in complex scenes. CC BY-NC 4.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ll-13/FIT-RS).
- **GeoChat_Instruct** (CVPR 2024, arXiv 2311.15826). 318k RS instructions drawn from LRBEN, NWPU captions, SOTA, SIOR and FAST. Apache-2.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MBZUAI/GeoChat_Instruct).
- **VRSBench** (NeurIPS 2024 D&B per common citation, unverified). 29,614 images, 52,472 object referrals and VQA pairs; the card prints the count as "3123,221", an evident typo, probably 123,221. GPT-4V-generated and human-verified, with train/val annotations and CC BY 4.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xiang709/VRSBench).
- **HRVQA** (ISPRS J. P&RS 2024, arXiv 2301.09460). 53,512 aerial images at 1024×1024 from Dutch open data at 8 cm GSD, with 1,070,240 semi-automatically generated QA. CC BY-NC 4.0 per the DANS record — [search summary of arXiv/DANS](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2301.09460). A later paper says the synthesis introduced annotation errors and derived a cleaner 50,019-sample subset, HRVQA-VL — [UAV-VL-R1, arXiv 2508.11196](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.11196v1).
- **GEOBench-VLM** (arXiv 2411.19325). More than 10,000 manually verified instructions covering counting large numbers of objects, tiny objects, fine-grained categorization and temporal analysis. GPT-4o reaches about 40% on MCQs — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/aialliance/GEOBench-VLM). No license tag.

#### (d) General perception benchmarks named in the brief (mostly low resolution)

- **MMStar**: 1,500 vision-indispensable MC samples, 6 capabilities × 250 each. No HF license tag — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Lin-Chen/MMStar).
- **BLINK**: per-task `val` and `test` splits of about 120–150 each (e.g. Counting 120/120, Art_Style 117/117, Spatial_Relation 143/143). Apache-2.0 — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BLINK-Benchmark/BLINK) (datasets-server).
- **CV-Bench**: 2,638 test items (2D 1,438, 3D 1,200), with fields `source_dataset, target_class, target_size`. Apache-2.0 — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nyu-visionx/CV-Bench).
- **SEED-Bench-2-Plus**: 2.3K MC questions on charts, maps and webs. CC BY-NC 4.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AILab-CVC/SEED-Bench-2-plus).
- **HallusionBench**: [rayguan/HallusionBench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/rayguan/HallusionBench), with `HallusionBench.json` and no license tag.
- **MMVP**: MIT, under 1K items — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MMVP/MMVP).

#### Method papers with no new labelled training set (checked)
- **DC²** is training-free; its paper's only dataset is HR-Bench — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DreamMr/HR-Bench).
- **ZoomEye** is training-free. Its HF repo [omlab/zoom_eye_data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/omlab/zoom_eye_data) contains only `annotation_mme-realworld.json` and a zip, i.e. evaluation material.
- **DyFo** (CVPR 2025 Highlight) is training-free. Its HF package contains only evaluation data (POPE-COCO/A-OKVQA/GQA and V\*). Apache-2.0 — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/oking0197/Dyfo).
- **InternLM-XComposer2-4KHD** (NeurIPS 2024) trains at up to 4KHD by changing patch layouts, because high-resolution data is scarce. I found no released dedicated high-resolution training set — [NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/4b06cdddb1cde6624c0be1465c7b800f-Abstract.html).
- **HEE** (arXiv 2607.00816) is a training-free method evaluated on HR-Bench, VisualProbe and MME-RealWorld — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.00816).

- Dense captions (not VQA): [MasterHow/ZoomCap-450K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MasterHow/ZoomCap-450K) has 447,285 captions and no images, joins to ScaleCap-450k images, and is CC BY-NC 4.0 ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MasterHow/ZoomCap-450K)). Its base is [long-xing1/ScaleCap-450k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/long-xing1/ScaleCap-450k) (CC BY 4.0 tag).

[ThinkMorph/Visual_Search](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ThinkMorph/Visual_Search) and the Agents-X visual_search sets are tool-trajectory style.
- Other RS VQA mirrors: RSVQA-HR/LR ([dmarsili/RSVQA-HR-2k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/dmarsili/RSVQA-HR-2k), CC BY 4.0; [cpratikaki/RSVQA-HR_qwen_finetuning](https://huggingface.co/datasets/cpratikaki/RSVQA-HR_qwen_finetuning)), EarthVQA mirrors ([Prakratiii-28/earthvqa](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Prakratiii-28/earthvqa)), [AdaptLLM/remote-sensing-VQA-benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/AdaptLLM/remote-sensing-VQA-benchmark), [xiang709/VRSBench-SAR](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xiang709/VRSBench-SAR) and [torchgeo/dior](https://huggingface.co/datasets/torchgeo/dior) (detection only).
- Scene text / documents: TextVQA-derived VCoT subsets inside Q-Zoom and Visual-CoT, covered by other researchers.
- Art: HRScene `realworld_ArtBench` and BLINK `Art_Style`; see Q5.
- Not relevant despite the name: [MaxwellWen/MME-CC](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaxwellWen/MME-CC) (chart modification / cognitive tasks).

### Inferences
- Labelled high-resolution *training* data for natural images is sparse and mostly from 2025–2026. The largest sources whose answers can be verified are ZwZ-RL-VQA (37k–111k), TreeVGR-RL-37K, DeepEyes-47k (22k of it fine-grained), Thyme-RL (55k), PixelReasoner-RL (15k), FineRS-4k (about 9k train) and VisualProbe (about 3–4k). The only 8K-class real training sets are remote sensing (SuperRS-VQA, UHR-BAT-SFT-10K).
- Much of the "V\* training data" lineage (SEAL → DeepEyes → TreeVGR → Q-Zoom's V\*-COCO 44K) uses COCO/GQA images at roughly 640 px. Its value is small-object and absent-object supervision, not real 4K resolution. This is inferred from the COCO sourcing; I did not measure image sizes.
- Several datasets reuse each other's images: V\*-train appears in DeepEyes, TreeVGR and Q-Zoom; DeepEyes 8k is reused in Mini-o3 RL. Mixing them needs deduplication by question and image.

### Gaps
- TreeVGR-SFT-35K composition: its HF card is a copy of the TreeBench card.
- HR-Bench exact QA count: the card says 100 FSP + 100 FCP "samples"; third-party copies are named "hr-bench-8k-800". Its actual license terms are also missing (empty LICENSE file).
- VisualProbe train count: 4,000 in the paper vs 2,900 HF rows. ZwZ count: 37k on the card vs 110,988 rows.
- GeoLLaVA-Data, VisualProbe, CoF-RL, MMStar, HallusionBench, V\* and SEAL data have no HF license tag. I did not check the GitHub repo licenses for all of them.
- Venues not confirmed: HRScene, HR-Bench (AAAI 2025?), VRSBench (NeurIPS 2024 D&B?).
- I could not find any public labelled VQA set built on the PANDA gigapixel dataset beyond HRScene's ~300-item `realworld_PANDA` slice.

## Q2. Task types and answer formats

### Takeaway
MC (A–D) dominates the benchmarks: V\*, HR-Bench, MME-RealWorld, TreeBench, XLRS-Bench and UR-Bench. The newer training sets lean towards short open-ended answers: DeepEyes converted everything to open-ended, while VisualProbe and FineRS mix MC and open-ended. Explicit counting and OCR appear in MME-RealWorld, TreeBench, HR-Bench, RS sets and ZwZ. No dataset I found systematically builds the paired yes/no questions with logical opposites that VisualOverload uses.

### Cited Findings
- V\*: MC with 4 options for attributes and 2 options for spatial relations — [arXiv 2312.14135v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14135v2). The 2-option format maps directly onto VisualOverload's A/B (2-option) choice questions — [VisualOverload card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/paulgavrikov/visualoverload).
- HR-Bench: FSP covers attribute recognition, OCR and visual prompting; FCP covers maps, charts and spatial relations — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DreamMr/HR-Bench).
- TreeBench: MC plus boxes; subcategories include Attributes, Material, Physical State, Object Retrieval, OCR (68 items), Ordering, Contact/Occlusion and Comparison — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeVGR-RL-37K).
- DeepEyes: "All questions are converted to an open-ended format"; answers go in `<answer>` tags — [arXiv 2505.14362](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14362).
- VisualProbe: free-form answers that need semantic matching (LLM-judge reward) — [arXiv 2509.07969](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07969).
- FineRS-4k: segmentation masks and boxes, MC VQA and open-ended VQA — [arXiv 2510.21311](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21311v1).
- ZoomBench / ZwZ: MCQ plus open questions, with bbox evidence — [ZoomBench card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZoomBench); [ZwZ card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZwZ-RL-VQA).
- Visual-CoT: QA plus a bbox for the key region, sometimes with reasoning steps — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/deepcs233/Visual-CoT).
- SEAL: includes 100k "negative" questions about absent or very small objects — [arXiv 2312.14135v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14135v2).
- MME-RealWorld: MC (`multi-choice options` field in derived data — [xinyu1205 viewer](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xinyu1205/mme_realworld_train_21690)). Includes counting, e.g. 133 vehicles in monitoring — [GitHub](https://github.com/yfzhang114/MME-RealWorld).
- XLRS-Bench: MC (A–D), plus multi-answer land-use classification, grounding and captioning — [GitHub](https://github.com/AI9Stars/XLRS-Bench).
- RSHR-Bench: MC, open VQA, grounding, counting, color and shape — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/RL-MIND/RSHR-Bench).
- UR-Bench: 4-option single choice — [arXiv 2601.08748v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08748v1).
- PixMo-Count: integer counts (train via points; eval counts 2–10) — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/pixmo-count).

### Inferences
- To mirror VisualOverload's format mix, convert open-ended training answers (DeepEyes, VisualProbe, ZwZ open items) into 4-way MC with distractors where possible. Keep counting and OCR items as freeform short answers.
- The logical-opposite yes/no pairs can be synthesised from existing labelled items. Each attribute or spatial item in V\*-train/SEAL (VAW attributes, COCO spatial relations) or TreeVGR can be turned into an affirmative/negated 2-option pair. SEAL's 100k negative/absent-object questions are a natural source for "No" answers. This is an inference, not an existing dataset.

### Gaps
- I did not inspect per-dataset answer-length distributions or how many items are counting/OCR in ZwZ, Thyme-RL or PixelReasoner-RL.

## Q4. How well does each match VisualOverload (dense scenes, detail perception, question types)?

### Takeaway
The closest matches in content are dense natural or aerial scenes with tiny targets and short verifiable answers: TreeVGR-RL-37K (dense SA-1B plus VisDrone), ZwZ-RL-VQA (micro-region details on >1K images, MC and open), FineRS-4k (4K drone frames, tiny objects, MC and open), VisualProbe (4K, small targets, dense distractors), and MME-RealWorld's monitoring/OCR/RS subsets, which are a benchmark. None depicts paintings, so there is a domain gap in style for every candidate. VisualOverload's "activity" and "scene" categories (people doing things in crowded narrative scenes) are thinly covered by all high-resolution datasets, which focus on objects, attributes, OCR and spatial relations.

### Cited Findings
- VisualOverload asks "simple, knowledge-free vision tasks in densely populated (or, overloaded) scenes". The best of 37 models (o3) gets 19.6% on the hard split and 69.5% overall. Failure modes are counting, OCR and logical inconsistency — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/paulgavrikov/visualoverload).
- TreeBench/TreeVGR prioritise "images with dense objects" from SA-1B and "focused visual perception of subtle targets in complex scenes" — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeBench). TreeVGR-RL adds VisDrone, a drone dataset of dense small objects (inferred from the file name) — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeVGR-RL-37K).
- VisualProbe: "small targets, numerous distractor objects, and high-resolution images" — [arXiv 2509.07969](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07969).
- FineRS-4k: XXS objects occupy <0.017% of the image area in 3840-wide frames — [arXiv 2510.21311](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21311v1).
- ZwZ targets cases where "decisive visual evidence is small, subtle, or easily overwhelmed by global context" — [ZoomBench card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZoomBench).
- MME-RealWorld covers counting at large scale (133 vehicles) and >5000×5000 RS — [GitHub](https://github.com/yfzhang114/MME-RealWorld).
- HR-Bench 8K is about 8K, averaging larger than VisualOverload's ~4K target — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DreamMr/HR-Bench).
- V\* images average 2246×1582, smaller than VisualOverload's ~8.3 MP — [arXiv 2312.14135v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14135v2).
- XLRS-Bench (8,500² average) and SuperRS-VQA (8,376² average) exceed VisualOverload in resolution but are nadir remote-sensing imagery — [XLRS GitHub](https://github.com/AI9Stars/XLRS-Bench); [GeoLLaVA GitHub](https://github.com/MiliLab/GeoLLaVA-8K).
- UR-Bench's "Humanistic Scenes" are long Chinese narrative scroll paintings with dense figures, the closest *content* analogue to VisualOverload. Their labels and release are unverified — [arXiv 2601.08748v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08748v1).
- HRScene's `realworld_ArtBench` subset gives a small set of artwork high-resolution QA (44 testmini, 30 val, 232 test with hidden labels) — HRScene datasets-server `/info` ([HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wenliang04/HRScene)).

### Inferences
Match ranking for VisualOverload (H = high, M = medium, L = low):

| Dataset | Dense scene | ~4K resolution | Q-type overlap (attr/count/OCR/activity/scene) | Overall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TreeVGR-RL-37K | H (SA-1B dense, VisDrone) | M | attr, spatial, some OCR | H |
| ZwZ-RL-VQA | M–H | M (>1K) | attr, OCR, count (MC+open) | H |
| FineRS-4k | H (aerial crowds of small objects) | H (3840 px) | attr/existence/count-like MC+open | M–H (aerial view) |
| VisualProbe | H | H (4K) | object search/attr, open | M–H |
| DeepEyes-47k (22k V\*) | M | L (COCO) | attr/spatial open | M |
| SEAL VQA data | M | L | attr, spatial, absence (good for yes/no) | M |
| Visual-CoT | L–M | L–M | broad incl. OCR | M |
| MME-RealWorld (benchmark) | H (monitoring, AD, RS) | M–H | count, OCR, attr | H, but dev only |
| HR-Bench 4K/8K | M | H | attr, OCR, spatial | dev only |
| TreeBench / ZoomBench / V\* | H / M / M | M | attr, OCR, spatial | dev only |
| HRScene val (incl. ArtBench, PANDA) | M–H | H | mixed | dev only |
| GeoLLaVA SuperRS/HighRS, UHR-BAT | H (objects) | very H | count, color, attr | M (domain gap) |

| MMStar, BLINK, CV-Bench, HallusionBench, SEED-2-Plus, MMVP | L | L | general | L (sanity eval only) |

- Activity recognition (what figures are doing) is the most underserved VisualOverload category. Of the catalogued datasets only general VQA mixes (Visual-CoT's GQA/general VQA parts, ZwZ's Visual Genome sources) touch human actions. I did not find a high-resolution, dense-crowd action-QA training set; this belongs in the gaps below.
- Counting at VisualOverload's scale (many figures in one painting) is best mirrored by MME-RealWorld monitoring, VisDrone-derived items and RS counting (RSHR, GEOBench-VLM). PixMo-Count only covers counts up to 10 in eval.

### Gaps
- I did not compute any quantitative domain similarity, such as CLIP-embedding distance between dataset images and VisualOverload paintings.
- I found no dataset of dense paintings with public VQA labels apart from small slices (HRScene-ArtBench) and possibly UR-Bench, whose release is unverified.

## Q5. Risk of image overlap with VisualOverload (public-domain paintings from Google Arts & Culture)

### Takeaway
The risk is negligible for photo- and remote-sensing-based sets (SA-1B, COCO/GQA, VisDrone, DOTA/FAIR1M, Dutch aerial, drone/YouTube footage). It is non-zero for web-scraped sources that may contain reproductions of famous public-domain paintings: ZwZ (LAION, MetaCLIP, CC12M), ScaleCap/ZoomCap (LAION/ShareGPT4V), PixMo-Count (web URLs), HRScene `realworld_ArtBench`, BLINK `Art_Style` and UR-Bench's scroll paintings. VisualOverload's images are public, so the risk can be removed by perceptual-hash or CLIP near-duplicate filtering against the 150 paintings.

### Cited Findings
- VisualOverload uses 150 public-domain paintings from museum collections on Google Arts & Culture — [arXiv 2509.25339](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.25339). The images ship in the public test parquet — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/paulgavrikov/visualoverload).
- ZwZ source images: SA-1B, LAION, MetaCLIP, Visual Genome, CC12M, STPLS3D — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZwZ-RL-VQA).
- TreeBench and V\* images come from SA-1B — [TreeBench card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeBench); [V\* paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.14135v2). SEAL/DeepEyes V\*-train comes from COCO2017/GQA — [arXiv 2505.14362](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14362).
- HRScene contains an ArtBench subset — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wenliang04/HRScene). BLINK has an Art_Style task (117 val / 117 test) — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/BLINK-Benchmark/BLINK).
- UR-Bench's Humanistic Scenes are scroll paintings collected from Wikipedia — [arXiv 2601.08748v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08748v1).
- PixMo-Count images are web URLs — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/pixmo-count).
- FineRS-4k frames come from YouTube and the authors' own UAV footage — [arXiv 2510.21311](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21311v1).

### Inferences
- The HRScene ArtBench slice and BLINK Art_Style are evaluation-only, so overlap there matters less for contamination than for reporting. Still, check them if they are used as dev sets.
- ArtBench-10 is, as I recall, sourced from WikiArt, which hosts many public-domain old-master paintings; if so, overlap with VisualOverload's Google Arts & Culture pictures is plausible. This is unverified.
- Recommended practice: compute pHash plus CLIP near-duplicates between every training image and VisualOverload's 150 images (with tiled crops, since training sets may hold crops of the same painting). Drop any matches and report the procedure.

### Gaps
- I did not run an actual overlap check on any dataset.
- ArtBench's image source and resolution inside HRScene were not verified.

## Q6. Fit for SFT, RL (verifiable answers), or dev/eval only

### Takeaway
For RL with verifiable rewards on Qwen2.5-VL, the best ready-made pools are TreeVGR-RL-37K (answers plus boxes), ZwZ-RL-VQA, DeepEyes-47k (V\* part), CoF-RL, PixelReasoner-RL, Thyme-RL and FineRS-4k. Each was built for or used with Qwen2.5-VL-7B-class models. For SFT, add Visual-CoT (bbox-grounded), SEAL VQA data, the GeoLLaVA SuperRS/HighRS pairs and UHR-BAT for extreme resolution. Use V\*, HR-Bench, MME-RealWorld(-Lite), TreeBench, ZoomBench, HRScene val/testmini, XLRS-lite, LRS-VQA and RSHR-Bench only as dev/eval sets. MME-RealWorld has been used as training data by MGPO, which disqualifies it as an eval set for any model trained that way.

### Cited Findings
- DeepEyes RL reward = accuracy + format + a conditional tool bonus. Training samples were difficulty-filtered against Qwen2.5-VL-7B — [arXiv 2505.14362](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.14362).
- Mini-o3 RL uses the VisualProbe train split plus 8,000 DeepEyes-47k examples, with Qwen2.5-VL-7B as the base model and an LLM-judge reward — [arXiv 2509.07969](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.07969).
- TreeVGR is initialized from Qwen2.5-VL-7B and trained with RL that supervises localization and reasoning jointly — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeBench). Its RL data carries `answer` and `target_instances` (boxes) — [HF viewer](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HaochenWang/TreeVGR-RL-37K).
- MGPO post-trains Qwen2.5-VL-7B on 21K MME-RealWorld samples — [arXiv 2507.05920](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.05920) (via search summary).
- ZwZ is explicitly "for training ... without test-time tool use". It is difficulty-filtered against Qwen3-VL-8B and has bbox fields — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/inclusionAI/ZwZ-RL-VQA).
- CoF-RL rows carry a `reward_model` / `groundtruth_complete` field (HF viewer) — [HF](https://huggingface.co/datasets/xintongzhang/CoF-RL-Data).
- PixelReasoner-RL: 15,402 queries "with verifiable answers" — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TIGER-Lab/PixelReasoner-RL-Data).
- Q-Zoom ships separate stage files for Qwen2.5-VL 3B and 7B — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/YuhengSSS/Q-Zoom-Training).
- GeoLLaVA-8K used its 81,367 pairs for SFT — [GitHub](https://github.com/MiliLab/GeoLLaVA-8K).
- HRScene publishes val (750) and testmini (1,000) for development, and keeps test labels hidden — [HF card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Wenliang04/HRScene).

### Inferences
- Suggested mix for VisualOverload, as a hypothesis to test:
  - RL: TreeVGR-RL-37K, the ZwZ MC/short-answer subset, FineRS-4k train MC/open, the VisualProbe train split (LLM-judge or MC-converted), the CoF-RL items whose answers can be checked by exact match, and the V\* portion of DeepEyes. Counting rewards are exact-integer; OCR rewards use normalized string or ANLS.
  - SFT warm-up: Visual-CoT (bbox → teaches "where to look"), SEAL VQA data (attribute, spatial and absence questions, convertible to yes/no pairs), and a small amount of SuperRS/HighRS for very-large-image handling.
  - Dev: MME-RealWorld-Lite, HR-Bench 4K, V\*, TreeBench, ZoomBench and HRScene val (incl. ArtBench and PANDA slices), after dedup against the training images.
- Several datasets are themselves tool-use trajectory sets (DeepEyes, CoF, PixelReasoner, Thyme, Mini-o3, DeepEyesV2), which other researchers cover. Their question/answer pairs can still be stripped of trajectories and used for plain single-pass SFT/RL.
- Qwen2.5-VL 3B/7B with native dynamic resolution can ingest about 4K inputs if max_pixels is raised. Training on 8K RS data needs tiling or token pruning (cf. GeoLLaVA-8K's background-token pruning). This is inferred from GeoLLaVA's motivation — [arXiv 2505.21375](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.21375v2).

### Gaps
- No published result I found reports fine-tuning on any of these sets and then evaluating on VisualOverload, so transfer is untested.
- MGPO's exact 21K split and its release status are unconfirmed.
- I did not verify how DeepEyes computes its accuracy reward (the paper does not describe it).
